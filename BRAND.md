# The APEX-8 name and badge

APEX-8's code is open source under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). The licence covers the code. It doesn't cover the APEX-8 name or the badge (the sun setting behind a snowy mountain over APEX-8, in `art/brand/`), which belong to jedi12many. They have these rules of their own.

## You may

- Show the badge on a game, demo or tool made with APEX-8, to say so: on its title screen, splash screen, box, store page or website. Use `art/brand/apex8.js` (the C64 badge), `art/brand/apex8-splash.kla` (the C64 splash) or `art/brand/apex8-source.png`, at any size.
- Convert it to fit a machine's colours and resolution, as the C64 versions do, as long as it stays recognisably the same badge.
- Say your game is "made with APEX-8" or "built on APEX-8", in words.

## You may not

- Change the badge's design: its shapes, its wording or its layout.
- Suggest that the APEX-8 project made, sponsored or endorses your game.
- Use the APEX-8 name or the badge as the name or logo of your own product.
- Call a fork or modified version of the engine APEX-8. A fork needs its own name; it can say it is "based on APEX-8".

If you want to do something these rules don't cover, ask by opening an issue.
