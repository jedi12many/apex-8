APEX-8

Game engines for the Commodore 64. Each browser version runs the same JavaScript reference that the C64 engine is checked against, frame for frame. They work with a keyboard, a gamepad or touch.

Play in the browser

The Drowned Crypt A small action game: clear the crypt with a pistol and sink the Mother of the Deep. With sound. Arrows or WASD to walk · hold Space to aim and fire · M mutes Green Hills The parallax platformer. Land on beetles, collect coins, reach the flag. With sound. Arrows or A/D to run · Space, Up, W or Z to jump · M mutes The Crypt The isometric dungeon engine, with a torchlight that follows the hero. Arrows or WASD to walk, two at once for diagonals The Dreadnought The vertical shoot-'em-up, with its sprite multiplexer and status bar. No sound in this version. Arrows or WASD to fly · Space, Ctrl or K to fire

R restarts or goes back to the title in every game.

Play on a C64, a C64 Ultimate or VICE

apex-8.d81 is a 1581 disk image with every C64 program behind a menu: the raycaster, the platformer, the dungeon, the shooter, its brick look, the sound players and the splash. Load it with LOAD"*",8 then RUN, and press a number to start a program. On a C64 Ultimate, mount and run it from a USB stick.

Set the machine to PAL, no turbo, with a 6581 SID. The single programs are also in c64/out.
